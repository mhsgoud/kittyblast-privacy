Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 July 2026 · Package: com.kittyblast.game

This Privacy Policy explains how Kitty Blast (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our Android game published on Google Play.

Short version: we store your optional display name and game scores for the leaderboard, show ads via Google AdMob, and keep progress on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Kitty Blast is a free-to-play puzzle game (with ads). Contact: shelftrack.support@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Display name — an optional nickname you enter for the global ranks / leaderboard.
  • Gameplay data — such as high score, max level reached, and kittens freed, used to show rankings and improve the experience.
  • Device / app identifiers — a random app-generated device id used to update your leaderboard row, plus advertising identifiers processed by our ads partner (see below).
  • Local game progress — scores, unlocked levels, settings, and similar data stored on your device (for example via app preferences).

We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

3. How we use information

  • Provide core game features (progress, settings, ranks).
  • Sync and display optional leaderboard scores.
  • Show advertisements that help keep the game free.
  • Respond to support or feedback requests you send us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads (for example banner ads). Google may collect and process device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, and interaction data related to ads, according to Google’s policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example Google settings → Ads, or the system advertising ID / privacy controls on your Android version).

5. Leaderboard / cloud sync

If you use Save & Sync / RANKS, we send your display name, scores, and device id to our backend (hosted with Supabase) so rankings can be shown to other players. Do not put sensitive personal data in your display name.

6. Sharing

We share data only as needed to operate the App:

  • Google AdMob / Google — advertising and related measurement.
  • Infrastructure providers — e.g. Supabase hosting for leaderboard storage.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Data retention

  • Local progress remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Leaderboard entries are kept while the service is offered, or until you request deletion.
  • Ad partners retain data under their own policies.

8. Children’s privacy

Kitty Blast is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices & deletion

  • Change or clear your display name in the App.
  • Reset local progress from Settings (where available) or by clearing app storage / uninstalling.
  • Request deletion of your leaderboard data by emailing shelftrack.support@gmail.com with your display name and approximate device / install details.

10. Security

We use HTTPS for network requests. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a non-identifying display name.

11. International users

If you play from outside the country where our servers or partners operate, your information may be processed in other countries that may have different data-protection laws.

12. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” above will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
